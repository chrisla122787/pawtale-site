Questions

What kind of photo should I upload?

One clear photo where your pet's face is fully visible, in good light, with no blur and no other animals in front. A phone photo is fine — the clearer the face, the better the likeness.

When will I receive my portraits?

We'll email your private, watermarked proof within 24 hours after receiving your payment and pet photo. Once you approve the proof, we'll send your final high-resolution digital files.

What if the portraits don't look like my pet?

That's exactly what the Pet Likeness Promise covers: request up to two free regenerations, and if you still don't approve the result, we refund your full purchase price.

Can I print the portraits?

Yes. Files are print-ready at 300 DPI — Standard includes an 8×10 inch file, and Premium adds 5×7, 11×14, and 16×20 files. Print them at home or at any photo shop, and frame them like any portrait.

What's the difference between Standard and Premium?

Both include one pet in all three art styles, a watermarked proof, two free regenerations, and the Likeness Promise. Premium adds more print sizes (5×7, 11×14, 16×20) and gift layouts (portrait card, square, and phone-story formats).
